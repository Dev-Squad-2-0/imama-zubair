# Short Summart (5 Insights):

**1. Best Teams for Sponsors:** **Geelong Cats** and **West Coast Eagles** have played the most matches in history. Putting za brand logo on their stadiums or TV streams guarantees the highest number of fan views.

**2. The Age 30 Retirement Wall:** Most players leave the league or retire around **29 to 30 years old**. Team managers must constantly recruit young draft talent early so the team stays strong when older players step down

**3. Uniform Player Sizes:** Player weights across all teams are almost exactly the same (around **85–90 kg**). Because body size is so standardized, coaches should focus training on speed and skill rather than trying to make players bigger. There are a few outliers but the number is minute.

**4. Most Exciting Teams for Fans:** **Hawthorn Hawks** and **GWS Giants** players score the highest average fantasy points per game. Ssports news sites should feature these teams on their homepages to get the most fan clicks.

**5. Safe Data Starting Point:** Player data from before the year **2000** is highly incomplete and missing many files. Data teams should only use stats from 2000 onward to make sure their player predictions are accurate
